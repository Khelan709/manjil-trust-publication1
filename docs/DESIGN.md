## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, scholarly, and refined digital environment tailored for academic researchers, literary scholars, peer-review committees, university librarians, and independent authors. Inspired by historic academic presses and modern scientific repositories, the brand identity balances deep institutional gravity with contemporary clarity and effortless digital navigation.

### Visual Mood & Movement
The design language adopts a **Modern Editorial / Institutional Scholarly** style. It combines structured layout grids, subtle hairline dividers, and dignified serif headlines with crisp geometric sans-serif interfaces. The experience evokes rigorous intellectual heritage, trust, transparency, and research archival excellence without feeling antiquated or dense. Whitespace is deliberate and calibrated, transforming high-density scholarly indices, metrics, and journal rosters into effortlessly scannable reading corridors.

## Colors

The color palette centers on classical academic prestige, high-legibility document contrast, and scholarly clarity:

- **Primary (`#0D2C54` - Deep Oxford Navy):** Anchors headers, primary call-to-actions, institutional banners, and key navigational surfaces. Conveys uncompromised authority and academic stability.
- **Secondary (`#C59B27` - Heritage Gold / Amber):** Highlights badges, peer-review milestones, open-access status, journal impact factors, and secondary actions (such as manuscript submission and subscription highlights).
- **Tertiary (`#702632` - Archival Burgundy):** Reserved for humanities journals, editorial board highlights, special literary monograph tags, and critical citations.
- **Neutral (`#1E242B` - Ink Charcoal):** Replaces pure black to deliver soft, bookish contrast for body copy and dense abstract reading.
- **Canvas & Surface Tiering:**
  - Base canvas: `#FBFBF9` (Warm Parchment White).
  - Surface cards: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Paper).
  - Subtle borders: `#E2E5EA` (Cold Stone Gray) with hairline accents.
  - Subdued fills: `#F0F4F8` for metadata badges and index pill counters.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a deliberate dual-family pairing:
1. **Source Serif 4 (Headlines & Editorial Accents):** Imparts the tactile presence of hot-metal typesetting, scholarly monographs, and university press journals. It grounds section introductions, publication titles, and manifesto statements in established prestige.
2. **Work Sans (Interface, Body, Labels, & Metadata):** A clean, structural neo-grotesque that ensures impeccable legibility across indexing registries, citation indices, tables of contents, DOI numbers, and administrative dashboards.

All uppercase micro-labels and pill category tags (e.g., `PEER-REVIEWED`, `OPEN ACCESS`, `ISSN`, `IMPACT FACTOR`) receive tracked letter-spacing (`0.05em` to `0.08em`) to guarantee quick recognition on editorial grids.

## Layout & Spacing

The system enforces a 12-column responsive grid on desktop with a maximum content canvas of 1280px, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet (768px–1024px) and a 4-column layout on mobile viewports (<768px).

### Layout Rhythms
- **Journal Catalog Grid:** 4 columns on wide screens (3 journals per row) or 3 columns (4 journals per row) for compact directories. Each journal item maintains generous vertical card spacing and an explicit 3:4 book-cover cover aspect ratio.
- **Section Pacing:** Major editorial transitions use `space-xxl` (64px) margin bounds, maintaining calm pacing between discovery zones, journal catalogs, affiliation rosters, and submission portals.
- **Micro-Gaps:** Compact 8px (`space-sm`) to 16px (`space-md`) bounds are strictly maintained inside card content groups (e.g., between ISSN badges, journal names, and action buttons).

## Elevation & Depth

To convey academic permanence and structural reliability, this design system avoids floating glass effects and exaggerated synthetic blurs in favor of **Tonal Paper Layers and Architectural Low-Contrast Outlines**.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FBFBF9` parchment background with hairline `#E2E5EA` borders for thematic dividers.
- **Level 1 (Scholarly Card):** `#FFFFFF` surface bordered with a crisp `1px solid #E2E5EA` frame and a quiet ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(13, 44, 84, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active / Hover Journal Card):** Slightly lifted state using `0 8px 20px rgba(13, 44, 84, 0.08)` accompanied by a subtle `#0D2C54` border tint to signal tactile selection without jarring movement.
- **Level 3 (Modal / Search Palette / Drawer):** Elevated review tools and index search dialogs feature `0 16px 36px rgba(13, 44, 84, 0.14)` with a deep Oxford Navy scrim (`rgba(13, 44, 84, 0.45)`).

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette relies on **Level 1 (Soft)** roundedness. Academic documentation demands straight, crisp, bookish edges with just enough corner softening to remain modern and tactile:

- **Cards, Catalog Tiles, and Containers:** `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, preserving the crisp rectilinear silhouette of bound books and printed journals.
- **Buttons and Inputs:** `0.25rem` (4px) to retain structural rigor.
- **Status Pills and Badges:** `0.75rem` (12px) pill contour specifically reserved for open-access indicators, indexing stamps, and metrics tags to visually distinguish metadata from clickable rectangular buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Submit Manuscript"):** Deep Oxford Navy background (`#0D2C54`), white text, crisp 4px corners, accompanied by an explicit trailing external/arrow icon. Hover state transitions smoothly to `#091F3C`.
- **Secondary / Highlight (e.g., "Subscribe", "Download Catalog"):** Heritage Gold background (`#C59B27`) with dark ink text or white text with bold weight. Hover transitions to `#AB841E`.
- **Ghost / Institutional:** Transparent fill with `1.5px solid #0D2C54` border and navy text, turning `#F0F4F8` on hover.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Open Access Badge:** Subtle gold wash (`#FFF9E6`), border `1px solid #ECC968`, text `#7A5B00` with an amber status dot.
- **Peer-Reviewed Pill:** Light blue wash (`#EDF4FC`), border `1px solid #C4DAF5`, text `#0D2C54`, styled in uppercase tracked text (`label-sm`).
- **Citation & Impact Tag:** Monospace-aligned sans pill showing metrics clearly (e.g., `CiteScore 3.2 • Scopus Indexed`).

### Journal & Publication Cards
- **Structure:** Vertical flex container featuring a 3:4 aspect-ratio publication cover frame with a subtle 1px border.
- **Content:** Category pill on top, followed by publication title in Source Serif 4 (`headline-sm`), ISSN/indexing stats in Work Sans (`body-sm`), followed by a full-width or pinned primary action button ("Submit Manuscript" or "View Archive").

### Form Fields & Manuscript Search Inputs
- Clean white inputs with a 1px border of `#CFD6DE`, transitioning to a 2px `#0D2C54` outline with a faint amber highlight ring on focus. Helper text and citation examples appear in `#5C6672`.

### Trust & Institutional Partners Strip
- Dedicated monochromatic or muted badge strip for indexing bodies (Crossref, COPE, DOAJ, Scopus, NISO) set over a soft parchment-tinted container (`#F4F6F9`) with 48px standardized logo frames and quiet grayscale-to-navy hover interactions.
